Personal support, your way

Everyone experiences menopause differently. Sometimes it can help to have someone to talk to, ask questions and better understand what you are experiencing.

Lisa offers individual sessions where you can discuss your experiences, explore any concerns and receive guidance throughout your menopause journey.

What can you expect?

One-to-one sessions provide an opportunity to focus entirely on your individual needs in a friendly and supportive environment.

  • Time to discuss your personal experiences.
  • Guidance and information about menopause.
  • An opportunity to ask questions.
  • Support in understanding potential changes.
  • Information to help you feel more informed.

Sessions can be tailored around the topics that matter most to you.
