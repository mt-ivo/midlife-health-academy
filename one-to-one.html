One-to-One Menopause Coaching

Your hormones. Your questions. Your time.

Personalised support and education

Whether you're approaching perimenopause, experiencing menopause symptoms or simply looking to understand your hormones better, a one-to-one session provides a relaxed space to ask questions and explore the information that matters most to you.

Every session is confidential, friendly and tailored to your individual needs. Together we can discuss hormone health, symptoms, treatment options and the practical realities of navigating midlife.

What can we talk about?

Sessions are shaped around your questions and interests. Topics commonly include:

  • Understanding perimenopause and menopause.
  • Recognising and managing symptoms.
  • Hormone health and wellbeing.
  • HRT, including benefits and risks.
  • Common menopause myths and misconceptions.
  • Lifestyle factors that may support long-term health.
  • Understanding your options and next steps.

You don't need to be experiencing symptoms to benefit. Understanding your hormones before menopause can be just as valuable.

Professional, evidence-based support

Lisa is a Registered Nurse with more than 20 years of NHS experience, a British Menopause Society member and a qualified educator with specialist menopause training.

Her approach combines clinical knowledge with friendly, down-to-earth conversations that help make hormone health easier to understand.
