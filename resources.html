Recommended Resources

Finding reliable information about perimenopause and menopause can make a real difference to understanding your hormones and making informed choices.

Below are some podcasts and scientific papers recommended by MidLife Health Academy to help you explore the subject further.

Podcast: The Dr Louise Newson Podcast

Explore conversations and information about menopause, hormones and women's health.

Listen to the Podcast

Scientific Paper: Monteleone et al. (2018) — Symptoms of Menopause: Global Prevalence, Physiology and Implications

Nature Reviews Endocrinology

Covers the physiology, prevalence and impact of menopausal symptoms, including sleep, mood and cognitive changes.

Read the Paper

Scientific Paper: Santoro et al. (2021) — The Menopause Transition: Signs, Symptoms, and Management Options

Journal of Clinical Endocrinology & Metabolism

Particularly useful for explaining fluctuating hormones, perimenopausal symptoms and treatment options.

Read the Paper

Scientific Paper: Santoro, Epperson & Mathews (2015) — Menopausal Symptoms and Their Management

Endocrinology and Metabolism Clinics of North America

Covers hot flushes, vaginal dryness, painful intercourse, sleep problems, mood and cognition.

Read the Paper

Scientific Paper: Schierbeck et al. (2012) — Effect of Hormone Replacement Therapy on Cardiovascular Events in Recently Postmenopausal Women: Randomised Trial

BMJ

Examines cardiovascular outcomes in women starting HRT relatively early after menopause. Its findings are encouraging but do not establish that HRT prevents heart disease in all women.

Read the Paper

Scientific Paper: Cauley et al. (2003) — Effects of Estrogen Plus Progestin on Risk of Fracture and Bone Mineral Density: The Women's Health Initiative Randomized Trial

JAMA

Demonstrates improvements in bone density and reductions in fractures with the HRT regimen studied, although overall risks and benefits must also be considered.

Read the Paper
