Our Medical Disclaimer

MidLife Health Academy provides evidence-based education and information about perimenopause, menopause and hormonal health.

Sessions and website content are intended for educational purposes only and do not constitute personalised medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Although sessions are delivered by a Registered Nurse, they are not a substitute for a clinical consultation with your GP, nurse practitioner or another appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

We encourage all individuals to seek personalised medical advice regarding symptoms, investigations, medication and treatment decisions.

If you experience new, persistent or concerning symptoms, please seek medical assessment.

MidLife Health Academy does not provide medical diagnoses, prescriptions or individualised treatment plans.

Important Health Information

This page provides general education and does not replace individual medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Symptoms may have causes unrelated to menopause. Please consult an appropriate healthcare professional about persistent, new or concerning symptoms.
