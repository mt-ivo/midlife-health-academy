Menopause Parties

Bringing people together for an enjoyable and informative menopause experience.

Learn, connect and enjoy

Menopause Parties offer a more social and informal way to explore the menopause journey.

Bringing friends and family together creates an opportunity to learn more about menopause, discover useful information and enjoy a shared experience in a relaxed environment.

Whether you are looking to learn something new or simply enjoy an informative get-together, these gatherings aim to make menopause education more accessible and approachable.

What are Menopause Parties?

These gatherings provide an opportunity to bring people together in a friendly and welcoming setting.

  • An informal and social atmosphere.
  • Opportunities to learn about menopause.
  • Information and guidance from Lisa.
  • A chance to ask questions and explore topics.
  • An opportunity to share the experience with others.
