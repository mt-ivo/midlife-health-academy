Peri-Parties

Good friends. Great conversations. A little fizz.

Bring the menopause conversation home

Invite your friends round and I'll bring the menopause conversation to you.

Peri-Parties are relaxed, informal gatherings designed to make hormone health and menopause easier to discuss, understand and learn about together.

Whether you're approaching perimenopause, experiencing symptoms or simply curious about what the future may hold, a Peri-Party provides a welcoming environment to learn, laugh and ask questions.

What happens at a Peri-Party?

Every gathering is designed to be informative, approachable and enjoyable.

  • Friendly, nurse-led conversations about menopause.
  • Discussion of common symptoms and experiences.
  • Clear information about HRT, including benefits and risks.
  • Myth-busting and evidence-based education.
  • Plenty of opportunities to ask questions.
  • A relaxed and supportive atmosphere.

No question is too small and no topic is off limits. The aim is simply to create a safe, comfortable space where people can learn together.

What's included?

Alongside an engaging and informative session, every Peri-Party includes:

  • A bottle of prosecco.
  • A cheese board.
  • Evidence-based menopause education.
  • The opportunity to ask questions in a relaxed setting.

It's a fun and informal way to learn more about hormones, menopause and midlife health while enjoying a get-together with friends.
